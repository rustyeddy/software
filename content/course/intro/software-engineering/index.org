#+TITLE: What software engineering is really about
#+WEIGHT: 10
#+DRAFT: true
#+DESCRIPTION: The responsibilities of delivering production software.

* Learning objective
Understand the responsibilities of delivering production-quality
software regardless of who writes the code.
* Core principle (working outline)
- Software exists to solve customer problems and deliver value.
- Writing code is only one part of software development.
- Demonstrations versus production: reliability, security, maintainability
  and operation under real conditions.
- Managing complexity, uncertainty, quality and change.
- What AI changes: delegation, implementation speed and coordination.
- What AI does not remove: requirements, design, verification and
  accountability. A human or organization remains accountable.
* Practical example
Contrast a working demonstration with a service that customers depend
on, including what happens when something fails or requirements change.
* Common pitfalls and verification
To develop during lesson drafting.

* Exercise or reusable asset (where appropriate)
To decide during lesson drafting.

* Key takeaway
Producing code is not the objective; delivering useful, reliable
software is.
