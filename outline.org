#+title: Software Course Outline

* Overview

This is the living outline of a software course that I plan to sell.
We will refine it as lessons are developed and reviewed. It
is for anyone responsible for a team of developers delivering
production software to customers and clients: managers (such as a
small-company CTO), team leads, and senior developers who own
end-to-end delivery.

The course is a blueprint for starting and running the software
development process from beginning to end. A manager can hand it to a
lead, and a lead can use it as a checklist for managing a team of
developers.

It starts at the idea phase, then works through developing
requirements, building and delivering the software, and running it in
production. Feedback from production goes back into the next
iteration of the loop.

The modules describe activities, not sequential gates in a waterfall.
Apart from initial bootstrapping, planning, design, development,
delivery, operations and feedback overlap and repeat as the project
learns. Within that delivery loop, developers repeatedly take a task
through implementation, testing, review and integration. Reinforce
these two loops wherever relevant.

The primary goal is to lay out the fundamental, long-lasting truths of
building software. Throughout, the course also covers how those truths
apply to modern teams that use AI/LLMs at every level.

AI is treated as a working part of the development process, not a side
topic. The course teaches how to configure, manage, and interact with
AIs as well as humans at each stage, from planning through production.

Students should leave with tangible, reusable assets: examples,
checklists, templates, or working project artifacts as appropriate.
Choose specific assets during lesson development rather than fixing
all of them in this outline. See [[file:lesson-process.org][Lesson development process]].
