#+title: Lesson Development Process

* Purpose
Develop practical lessons incrementally. The living [[file:outline.org][course outline]]
guides the work; update it when we learn something that improves the
course. Keep the material focused on enduring software engineering
principles, with human and AI teams integrated throughout.

* Workflow
1. Select a lesson from the outline and create or reuse a GitHub issue.
2. Agree on the audience need, learning outcome, scope and practical
   takeaway. Choose an example, exercise or reusable asset where useful.
3. Draft the lesson and any supporting assets in Org format or an
   appropriate source format. Explain why the principle matters, how
   to apply it, and how it changes (or does not) with AI contributors.
4. Review via a pull request for technical accuracy, clarity, practical
   usefulness, scope and consistency with the course. Verify examples
   and assets; revise in response to review.
5. After human approval, merge, update the outline status and capture
   follow-up work. Incorporate student feedback in later iterations.

* Lightweight lesson template
- Title and learning outcome
- Core principle and why it matters
- Practical workflow or example (human and AI where relevant)
- Common pitfalls and verification
- Exercise, checklist item or reusable asset, where appropriate
- Key takeaway

* Definition of done
A lesson has a clear outcome, accurate and actionable content, reviewed
examples, and any promised student assets. It fits the delivery loop
without implying waterfall gates. The author and reviewer agree it is
ready for the current release; it can still evolve later.

Do not create extra lessons or mandatory artifacts simply to satisfy
this template. Keep lessons concise and let the outline evolve.
